# Project checkpoint - goals, progress and milestones

## 1. Goals

Our main goal is to develop an agent that will play the Quoridor board game and be as strong as possible. We want to try different approaches, including:

- algorithm similar to AlphaZero, based on Monte Carlo Tree Search and Neural Networks,
- reinforcement learning algorithms, such as Q-learning or SARSA.

## 2. Baselines and evaluation

To evaluate our agent's performance, we will use benchmark agents, such as BFS agent. We will also calculate metrics like win rate against the benchmark agents, average game length, and other relevant statistics to assess the effectiveness of our approaches.

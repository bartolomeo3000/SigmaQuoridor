# Quoridor rules

1. Two players on an n x n board.
2. Players start on opposite sides of the board.
3. The bottom player wins by reaching the top row, the top player wins by reaching the bottom row.
4. Each player has k walls at their disposal.
5. Walls have a length of 2 and can be placed in the grooves between the squares of the board, either horizontally or vertically, but the walls cannot cross.
6. It is not allowed to completely cut off oneself or the opponent from the goal.
